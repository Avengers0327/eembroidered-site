Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what we hear most. Don't see your question? Just give us a call or text.

Do you only do embroidery?

Despite the name, we can do so much more than embroidery! To name a few examples, we can glitter Christmas stockings or hats, and we can do screen-printing!

Can I place large orders?

Yes, you can place large orders! Just contact us at (201) 916-3221 or use the form on the website.

When are you open?

We operate on the hours of Paramus Park Mall, which is where we are located.

What can I customize?

We can customize almost anything you may need or want! From hoodies to mugs — we've got you covered.

Do you accept refunds or exchanges?

No, we do not accept refunds or exchanges, unless we have made a mistake. Otherwise, all purchases are final.

Can I bring my own items for you to customize?

Yes, you totally can!

Still have questions?

We're happy to talk it through — call, text, or send us a quote request.
