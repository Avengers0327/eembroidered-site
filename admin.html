Turn on tracking

      
Cloudflare Web Analytics is free, needs no cookie banner, and doesn't slow your site down.


      
    
        
  1. Make a free account at cloudflare.com, then open Web Analytics and add your site's address.
    2. 
        
  2. Cloudflare hands you a short snippet that looks like this, with your own token filled in:
          
    <script defer
  src="https://static.cloudflareinsights.com/beacon.min.js"
  data-cf-beacon='{"token": "YOUR_TOKEN_HERE"}'>
</script>
    
        
    3. 
        
  3. Paste that snippet just before the closing </body> tag on every page — index, about, work, testimonials, bulk, faq, and contact.
    4. 
        
  4. Wait about a day, then read your numbers on the Cloudflare dashboard.
    5.
